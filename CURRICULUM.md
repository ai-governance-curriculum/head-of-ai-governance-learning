# Head of AI Governance Curriculum

**Role level:** 60 (program-leadership role — AI Governance family)
**Family:** AI Governance
**Status:** planned — modules and projects below are the planned scope authored from [`.aicg/curriculum-plan.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json). Lessons and projects will be drafted by subsequent autonomous content cycles.

## Overview

This track teaches the *leadership execution* of an enterprise AI governance and risk program at level 60: consuming the [`senior-ai-governance-architect`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/senior-ai-governance-architect-learning) (level 50) operating design (control library, policy taxonomy, cross-jurisdiction crosswalk, ISO/IEC 42001 AIMS, risk taxonomy + appetite, assurance architecture, evidence architecture, third-party program, post-market surveillance architecture, GRC-for-AI reference architecture, operating model, sector blueprints) and *running the program*: chairing the AI governance council and adjacent committee cadences; owning the ISO/IEC 42001 AIMS operating calendar (internal audit rounds, management-review cadence, risk-treatment plan refresh, competence assessments, corrective-action follow-through) at ISO/IEC 42006 audit-body inspection quality; running the enterprise AI risk register at portfolio cadence; leading the third-party-audit interface (ISO 42001 certification body, ForHumanity IAAIS, BABL AI, third-line internal audit); authoring and delivering quarterly board / audit-committee / risk-committee / tech-committee AI-risk reporting (with IoD + NACD reference shapes); directly engaging regulators across the EU AI Office + EU AI Board + national CAs (EU AI Act Chapter VII), US AISI + AISIC + UK AISI + UK ICO, sector regulators (Fed / OCC / FDIC / OSFI / NAIC / FDA / MHRA / EMA / EEOC / FTC / CFPB / HHS OCR / CPPA / Colorado AG / state AGs), CISA + NCSC, and national CAs in Canada / Singapore / Australia / India / Korea / Brazil; owning EU AI Act Article 72 post-market monitoring cadence and Article 73 serious-incident filing at leadership scope; leading enterprise regulatory-change management at portfolio scope; leading the enterprise's external positioning (EU AI Office GPAI Code of Practice, G7 Hiroshima Code, White House Voluntary AI Commitments, Frontier Model Forum charter, Partnership on AI, MLCommons AI Safety Working Group, AISIC) and standards-body participation (ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42, CEN-CENELEC JTC 21, IEEE 7000-series, NIST public workshops, OECD.AI); designing and running the AI-governance program measurement system (KRIs, KPIs, maturity model, external benchmarking, ROI reporting to CFO); hiring, coaching, and career-pathing the AI-governance function across `ai-governance-analyst` (level 15) → `ai-risk-engineer` (level 25) → `ai-evaluation-engineer` (level 35) → `senior-ai-governance-architect` (level 50) → `head-of-ai-governance` (this role, level 60); leading cross-enterprise CxO coordination with CISO / CIO / CTO / CDO / CPO / DPO / CRO / CAE / CFO / GC / CHRO / Chief Compliance Officer / Chief Communications Officer; leading AI-incident response at enterprise level (coordinating with SOC + DFIR + Communications + Legal + Regulator Relations + Board); leading sector-program interfaces (banking with Federal Reserve + OCC exam coordination; insurance with NAIC + state DOI; health with FDA + HHS OCR + medical staff; pharma with FDA + MHRA + EMA + PMDA regulatory-submission leadership; public sector with OMB M-25-21 Federal Chief AI Officer designation + AI Governance Board seat + IG relations + CAIO Council participation; frontier lab with RSP / Preparedness / FSF program leadership + White House + AISI relations; critical infrastructure with CISA + NCSC + ENISA); positioning the certifications portfolio (IAPP AIGP baseline + ISO/IEC 42001 LI/LA + IAPP CIPP / CIPM / CIPT + ISACA CISM / CGEIT / CRISC + IIA CIA + ForHumanity Fellow + BABL AI Fellow) and community-leadership scope (working-group chair positions at ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42, CEN-CENELEC JTC 21, Frontier Model Forum, Partnership on AI, MLCommons AI Safety); owning the AI-governance function budget, vendor P&L (GRC-for-AI + AI-observability + AI-runtime security + assurance), and multi-year investment case to CFO / CEO / audit committee, coordinated with the Chief AI Officer (level 70) on the enterprise-wide AI budget envelope.

It is a **program-leadership track at level 60**, positioned in the **AI Governance family**, between the [Senior AI Governance / Risk Architect](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/senior-ai-governance-architect-learning) (level 50, architectural design authority) below and the [Chief AI Officer](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning) (level 70, executive AI strategy scope) above. The differentiator against the lower `senior-ai-governance-architect` (level 50) is *leadership execution ownership*: this curriculum runs the program, engages regulators directly, reports to the board, leads the function as a people leader, coordinates across the CxO layer, and owns the budget + vendor P&L + investment case — the architect designs; the head leads. The differentiator against the higher `chief-ai-officer` (level 70) is that this role does not own AI *strategy*, AI portfolio *P&L*, external positioning at *CEO / board-seat scope*, or the *enterprise-wide AI budget envelope* — where a CAIO designation exists, the head reports into (or matrixes with) that role; where it does not, the head negotiates directly with CIO / CTO / CDO / CFO.

Total planned commitment: **204 hours across 13 modules** + **210 hours across 4 projects** = **~414 hours**.

## Ownership rule

Following the project-wide ownership rule, this curriculum:

- **Owns** the *leadership execution* of an enterprise AI governance and risk program — running the program the architect designed, board / audit-committee / risk-committee reporting, direct regulator engagement, enterprise regulatory-change management, external positioning + voluntary-code sign-ons, program measurement + maturity, leading the governance function as a people leader, cross-enterprise CxO coordination, AI-incident response leadership at enterprise level, sector-program leadership, certifications + career-path + community-leadership scope, AI-governance function budget + vendor P&L + investment case.
- **Defers down** to [`senior-ai-governance-architect-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/senior-ai-governance-architect-learning) (level 50) for the architectural design authority the head consumes as the operating design of the program they lead; to [`ai-evaluation-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-evaluation-engineer-learning) (level 35, Governance family) for release-assurance / audit-trail / regulator-facing methodology depth (the head signs off; peer executes methodology); to [`ai-risk-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-risk-engineer-learning) (level 25) for the hands-on engineering craft of AI risk (the head sponsors and challenges; that role builds); to [`ai-governance-analyst-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-governance-analyst-learning) (level 15) for operational analyst legwork (the head staffs and directs; that role executes); to [`ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/ml-engineer-learning) (level 20) for classical ML fundamentals; to [`ai-infra-junior-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-junior-engineer-learning) (level 10) for engineering-craft prerequisites.
- **Defers sideways** to [`ai-eval-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-eval-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30, AI Engineering family) for application-layer LLM/agent evaluation-engineering depth; to [`model-evaluation-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/model-evaluation-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30, ML Engineering family) for benchmark / statistical / calibration methodology depth; to [`ai-infra-mlops-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-mlops-learning) (peer, level 25, Infra family) for MLOps automation into which the governance-runtime slice plugs.
- **Defers sideways-and-up** to [`agentic-safety-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/agentic-safety-engineer-learning) (level 40) for frontier-agent red-team methodology and dangerous-capability evaluation depth (the head sponsors and reviews the tripwire / rollback decisions).
- **Defers sideways** to [`security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/security-learning) and [`ai-infra-security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-security-learning) (level 35) for deep ML/AI security at platform scale (the head coordinates with CISO-scope peers on runtime defence integration).
- **Defers up** to [`chief-ai-officer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning) (level 70) for AI strategy, AI portfolio P&L, external positioning at CEO / board-seat scope, and the enterprise AI budget envelope.
- **Out of scope** to General Counsel and Chief Legal Officer (this role interfaces with them; GC delivers the legal opinion); to Chief Privacy Officer / DPO where privacy-scope is broader than AI; to Chief Risk Officer where enterprise-risk-scope is broader than AI (this role reports into or matrixes with the CRO in many enterprises); to Chief Compliance Officer where enterprise-compliance-scope is broader than AI; to Chief Audit Executive (this role coordinates with them; CAE owns third-line independence); to Chief Communications Officer (this role coordinates on public trust; CCO owns comms); to Security Operations / DFIR (this role designs the interface; SOC runs the runbook).

See [`JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md`](JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md) for the requirements-to-coverage map and the cited public references the catalog is grounded in.

## Module summaries

### mod-101 — Head of AI Governance Role Scope and Leadership Shape
Diagram this role's exact scope on the level ladder — what `ai-governance-analyst` (level 15) legwork, `ai-risk-engineer` (level 25) engineering craft, `ai-evaluation-engineer` (level 35) release-assurance methodology, `agentic-safety-engineer` (level 40) frontier-agent methodology, and `senior-ai-governance-architect` (level 50) architecture the head consumes; what `chief-ai-officer` (level 70) inherits or extends; and where the head's leadership authority begins and ends. Read NIST AI RMF 1.0 GOVERN function (with the Playbook) as the leadership shape for the second line. Read ISO/IEC 38507 as the definitional shape for board-facing responsibilities. Read the IIA Three Lines Model at leadership scope. Position ISACA COBIT 2019 and NIST CSF 2.0 GOVERN function as sibling references. Position the AI-governance function against CRO / CCO / CPO / CAE / GC / CISO / CIO / CTO / CDO / Chief Communications Officer / CAIO scopes. Author the engagement contract with `senior-ai-governance-architect` (level 50) below and `chief-ai-officer` (level 70) above.

### mod-102 — Running the AI Governance Program
Consume the senior-ai-governance-architect (level 50) operating design and translate it into a running program — chair the AI governance council, run adjacent committee cadences (audit, risk, technology, executive), own minute-keeping and decision-log discipline. Own the ISO/IEC 42001 AIMS operating calendar (internal audit rounds, management-review cadence, risk-treatment plan refresh, competence assessments, corrective-action follow-through, non-conformity closure) at ISO/IEC 42006 audit-body inspection quality. Run the enterprise AI risk register at portfolio cadence (ISO 31000 / ISO/IEC 23894 / ISO/IEC 27005 parents). Lead the third-party-audit interface (ISO/IEC 42001 certification body, ForHumanity IAAIS, BABL AI Algorithm Bias Auditor, sector regulator examiner, third-line internal audit). Run annual and multi-year maturity assessments against NIST AI RMF Playbook + ISO/IEC 42001 maturity dimensions + OWASP AI Security & Governance Checklist coverage. Own the AI system inventory quality bar and the AI supply-chain evidence discipline (CycloneDX ML-BOM + SPDX AI + SLSA + Sigstore + model / system / dataset cards) at program scope.

### mod-103 — Board, Audit Committee, and Risk Committee AI Reporting
Design the board-facing AI-risk report shape (heat maps, KRI dashboards, incident-summary decks, portfolio risk-appetite tracking, regulator-engagement summary, third-party-vendor status), using IoD AI-governance guidance and NACD AI Oversight Blueprint as reference shapes. Design the audit-committee AI-risk deep-dive shape (first-line effectiveness, second-line coverage, third-line audit findings and remediation status, external audit findings) aligned to IIA Three Lines + IIA CAE guidance. Design the risk-committee (or technology-committee) AI-risk shape (portfolio appetite calibration decisions, capability-tier escalations, stop-shipping triggers) layered onto COSO ERM + ISO 31000. Coordinate with CRO / CAE / GC / CISO on report content ownership before circulation. Author the reporting-pipeline discipline. Rehearse the difficult-conversation pattern (appetite breach, regulator engagement deteriorating, Article 73 investigation, stop-shipping decision). Draw shape lessons from frontier-lab tiered risk frameworks (Anthropic RSP, OpenAI Preparedness, Google DeepMind FSF).

### mod-104 — Direct Regulator Engagement
Read EU AI Act Chapter VII (Articles 64-70 — EU AI Office, EU AI Board, national competent authorities) as the leadership-scope engagement map. Own the EU AI Act Article 72 post-market monitoring filing cadence and Article 73 serious-incident reporting workflow at leadership scope. Design engagement patterns with US AISI + AISIC, UK AISI (with Inspect), and equivalents in Canada / Singapore / EU Member States. Design engagement patterns with sector regulators — Federal Reserve + OCC + FDIC (SR 11-7 + OCC 2011-12 + SR 23-4), OSFI (E-23 for Canadian FIs), NAIC + state DOIs (AI Model Bulletin), FDA + MHRA + EMA + Health Canada (GMLP + PCCP), EEOC + DOL (employment), FTC + CFPB (consumer), HHS OCR (HIPAA), CPPA (California ADMT), Colorado AG (Colorado AI Act), state AGs (state AI acts), UK ICO (data-protection interface for AI), CISA + NCSC (Secure AI System Development). Design the regulator-exam runbook at leadership scope. Own the public-consultation submission process (NIST AI RMF public comment, CEN-CENELEC JTC 21, CPPA rulemaking, FDA docket, EU AI Office GPAI Code work). Distinguish 'engaging a regulator' (this role's scope) from 'delivering a legal opinion' (General Counsel's scope).

### mod-105 — Enterprise Regulatory Change Management
Design the enterprise horizon-scanning cadence for AI regulation, coordinating with `ai-governance-analyst` (level 15) on tracking legwork and with legal counsel on legal-opinion interpretation. Design the interpretation-and-prioritisation workflow. Design the enterprise change-management plan for a regulatory change (comms cadence from CEO letter to team-level runbook update, training refresh, exception window management, board-report inclusion, regulator-facing notification where relevant). Manage the tension between speed (regulator's compliance date) and safety (enterprise's operational readiness). Lead coordination between senior-ai-governance-architect (level 50, updated crosswalk), product / engineering, legal, procurement, HR, and communications on rollout. Rehearse specific pending changes as of the research window — EU AI Act GPAI obligations timelines, CEN-CENELEC JTC 21 harmonised-standards publication dates, state AI act enforcement dates (Colorado 2026, Texas TRAIGA), Korea AI Basic Act, OSFI E-23 2027 effective date, evolving AISI methodology.

### mod-106 — External Positioning, Voluntary Codes, and Standards-Body Leadership
Design the internal decision process for signing on to a voluntary code — EU AI Office GPAI Code of Practice, G7 Hiroshima Process Code of Conduct, White House Voluntary AI Commitments where applicable, Frontier Model Forum charter where frontier-lab scope applies, Partnership on AI membership, MLCommons AI Safety Working Group participation, US AISI Consortium (AISIC) membership — with the CEO / GC / CRO / CFO sign-off flow explicit. Design the standards-body participation posture at leadership scope (ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42, CEN-CENELEC JTC 21, IEEE 7000-series, NIST public workshops, OECD.AI Expert Group). Design the public trust posture. Design the enterprise's RFI / RFP response posture. Rehearse the difficult external decision (declining, withdrawing, publicly disagreeing with a regulator's methodology). Draw shape lessons from frontier-lab external positioning (Anthropic RSP publication cadence, OpenAI Preparedness updates, Google DeepMind FSF cadence).

### mod-107 — Program Measurement, Maturity, and ROI Reporting
Design KRIs and KPIs for the AI-governance program at enterprise scope (control-testing pass rate, exception-waiver load, incident MTTR, board-report cycle time, regulator-response latency, third-party-vendor tier coverage, evidence-completeness rate, AIA turnaround time, maturity progression, cost-per-AI-system-governed). Author the enterprise AI-governance maturity model informed by NIST AI RMF Playbook + ISO/IEC 42001 + IAPP AIGP body-of-knowledge + MLCommons AI Safety + ForHumanity IAAIS. Design the annual maturity assessment (self-assessment, second-line challenge, third-line audit input, external benchmarking against peer institutions in-sector). Design the ROI reporting shape to CFO (avoided-loss, deal-enablement, trust-margin). Design the program-cost management discipline. Rehearse the difficult program-measurement conversation.

### mod-108 — Leading the AI Governance Function as a People Leader
Design the AI-governance function's org shape — analysts, engineers, evaluators, architects — with reporting-line decisions (directly reporting versus matrixed from product / engineering / risk / audit), level-ladder mapping (level 15 → 25 → 35 → 50 → 60), and succession-planning discipline. Design the hiring plan (multi-year headcount ladder, sourcing strategy including career switchers from audit / privacy / MRM / legal / product / engineering and IAPP AIGP / ISO/IEC 42001 LI cohorts). Design the coaching and mentoring cadence. Manage the second line's independence from first-line product without operational-distance failure. Coordinate with the Chief Audit Executive (IIA Three Lines + CAE guidance + IPPF) on the third-line AI audit programme. Own the leadership-scope vendor relationship at CxO account scope. Rehearse the difficult people-leadership conversation.

### mod-109 — Cross-Enterprise Leadership and CxO Coordination
Design coordination patterns with CISO (deep AI security handoff, red-team-finding routing into SOC signal), CIO / CTO / CDO (AI-platform ownership handoff, model-registry integration, data-governance interface), CPO / DPO (AIA / DPIA interface, ROPA interface, CPPA / GDPR / DPDPA obligations shared or divided), CRO (parent ERM framework, reporting line, appetite-tolerance-limit language), CAE (third-line audit programme, IIA Three Lines / CAE guidance / IPPF, audit-committee joint appearance rhythm), GC (regulator-obligation interpretation, litigation posture, SAR / MOU / MRA management), CFO (governance-function budget, ROI reporting, capital-planning), CHRO (AI-in-HR governance under NYC LL 144 / EEOC / Illinois HB 3773), Chief Compliance Officer (parent-compliance framework), Chief Communications Officer (public trust posture, media strategy, crisis-communications runbook). Chair the AI governance council. Manage the conflict pattern with product owners over stop-shipping decisions.

### mod-110 — AI-Incident Response Leadership
Lead the enterprise AI-incident response as the incident-command shape at leadership scope for AI-specific incidents (model failure, systemic bias event, prompt-injection compromise, model-extraction event, jailbreak-driven downstream harm, third-party model provider outage / withdrawal, dataset compromise, judge / evaluator corruption). Coordinate with SOC + DFIR (SOC runs the runbook; the head is on the incident bridge), Communications, Legal, Regulator Relations, Government Affairs, and the board. Own the EU AI Act Article 73 serious-incident filing at leadership scope. Own equivalent US federal / state / sector serious-incident disclosure (Colorado AI Act, NAIC bulletin, HHS OCR HIPAA breach, FDA MDR for AI-enabled medical device malfunctions, EEOC / DOL for AI-in-HR incidents, CPPA data-breach adjacencies). Lead the post-incident learning cycle at program scope. Draw calibration lessons from AIID + OECD.AI + MIT AI Risk Repository + OWASP LLM Top 10 + MITRE ATLAS.

### mod-111 — Sector Program Leadership
Banking — lead in the model-risk-management context: Federal Reserve + OCC + FDIC exam coordination on AI/ML model risk under SR 11-7 + OCC 2011-12 + SR 23-4; OSFI E-23 for Canadian FIs; interfacing with the enterprise Head of Model Risk Management; consumer-facing AI (CFPB) and fair-lending shape. Insurance — lead in the state-DOI + NAIC context: NAIC AI Model Bulletin adoption, Colorado Insurance Regulation, state DOI exam coordination, external actuarial coordination. Health system — lead in the FDA + HHS OCR context: FDA GMLP + PCCP submission leadership on SaMD, clinical-governance interface with the Chief Medical Officer, HIPAA breach notification for AI-triggered PHI events. Pharmaceutical — lead in the FDA + MHRA + EMA + PMDA context: regulatory-submission leadership on AI-enabled clinical trials, pharmacovigilance, manufacturing; GxP interface. Public sector / federal contractor — lead as the Federal Chief AI Officer designation under OMB M-25-21; AI Governance Board seat; IG relations; Federal CAIO Council participation; FedRAMP + NIST SP 800-53 + NIST AI RMF interface. Frontier lab — lead an RSP / Preparedness / FSF program; White House engagement; US AISI + UK AISI evaluation-access negotiation; industry-body chair roles; public-transparency cadence. Critical infrastructure — lead for a critical-infrastructure operator: CISA + NCSC + ENISA engagement. Design the sector-adaptation methodology at leadership scope.

### mod-112 — Certifications, Career Path, and Community Leadership
Position the certifications portfolio — IAPP AIGP (near-universal baseline), ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Auditor / Lead Implementer (required at leadership scope when running an AIMS), IAPP CIPP/US or CIPP/E + CIPM + CIPT (privacy adjacencies), ISACA CISM / CGEIT / CRISC (governance / risk / audit adjacencies), IIA CIA (internal-audit adjacency), ForHumanity Fellow (independent-audit adjacency), BABL AI Fellow (bias-audit adjacency) — with the sequencing decision (which to earn before promoting into head-of-role, which to earn after). Map career paths into head-of-AI-governance (from senior-ai-governance-architect, Head of MRM in banking, CCO / CPO / CAE with AI-heavy remit, senior legal counsel with AI-regulatory specialisation, Big Four AI-governance partner). Map career paths out of head-of-AI-governance (Chief AI Officer, cross-functional CxO promotions, board / non-executive-director seats, public-sector CAIO under OMB M-25-21, industry-body chair, academic-affiliation). Design the community-leadership scope (working-group chair positions at ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42, CEN-CENELEC JTC 21, Frontier Model Forum, Partnership on AI, MLCommons AI Safety, IAPP AIGP body-of-knowledge committee, IIA IPPF working groups, US AISI Consortium). Design the public speaking / thought-leadership discipline.

### mod-113 — Budget, Vendor P&L, and Investment Case
Own the AI-governance function budget line (headcount, vendor spend across GRC-for-AI + AI-observability + AI-runtime security + assurance, tooling depreciation, external-audit cost, training and certification cost, incident-response reserve, external-benchmarking cost, community-leadership cost) with a defensible multi-year plan. Author the multi-year investment case to CFO / CEO / audit committee (programme case, transformation case moving off spreadsheets onto a certifiable AIMS runtime, incident-preparedness case). Manage the GRC-for-AI vendor P&L at CxO scope (Credo AI, Holistic AI, ModelOp, Monitaur, ServiceNow AI Control Tower, IBM watsonx.governance, Fairly AI, Enzai, Trustible, Verify AI). Manage the AI-observability vendor P&L (Fiddler AI, Arthur AI, WhyLabs, Evidently). Manage the AI-runtime security vendor P&L (Robust Intelligence, Lakera Guard, Calypso AI, HiddenLayer, Protect AI, HydroX AI, Cranium, TrojAI). Manage the assurance-body relationship P&L (ISO/IEC 42001 certification body, ForHumanity IAAIS, BABL AI, Big Four audit engagement, specialist AI-audit firms). Build the ROI case with defensible modelling. Coordinate with the Chief AI Officer (level 70) on the enterprise-wide AI budget envelope.
