# Resources for mod-104 — Direct Regulator Engagement

Primary sources for the topics in this module. All links point to
official statute publishers, regulator landing pages, standards
bodies, or the professional associations that maintain the
standard. Avoid secondary summaries when authoring the artefacts
described in the exercises — a head of role who quotes primary
sources at the audit committee is a head of role the audit committee
ratifies.

## EU AI Act — the primary text and the Chapter VII engagement map

- [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — the EU AI Act (consolidated text via
  EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) — the
  consolidated legal text. Chapter VII (Articles 64–70) frames the
  Union-layer and Member-State layer engagement map in chapter 01.
  Article 72 (post-market monitoring) and Article 73 (serious-
  incident reporting) anchor chapter 02. Chapter IX (Articles 74–84,
  market surveillance) frames the examination regime in chapter 05.
- [European AI Office (EU AI Office landing page)](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-office)
  — the Commission-anchored Union-level body. Chapter 01, chapter 03
  (GPAI-adjacent), and chapter 06 (guidance and Code of Practice
  consultations).
- [European Artificial Intelligence Board](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-board)
  — the Article 65 Board of Member-State representatives. Chapter 01.
- [EU AI Office — General-Purpose AI Code of Practice](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-code-practice)
  — the Code of Practice work stream. Chapter 01 and chapter 06.
- [European Commission — Have Your Say portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say_en)
  — the Commission-wide public-consultation platform. Chapter 06.
- [Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 on market surveillance and compliance
  of products](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/1020/oj) —
  the general market-surveillance regulation the AI Act's Chapter IX
  market-surveillance regime rides on top of. Chapters 01 and 05.
- [CEN-CENELEC JTC 21 — Artificial Intelligence](https://www.cencenelec.eu/areas-of-work/cen-cenelec-topics/artificial-intelligence/)
  — the European standardisation committee producing the harmonised
  standards under the Commission's standardisation request for the
  AI Act. Chapter 01 (notified-body layer) and chapter 06
  (consultation portfolio).
