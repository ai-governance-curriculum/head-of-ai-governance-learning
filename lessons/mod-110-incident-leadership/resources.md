# Resources for mod-110-incident-leadership (AI-Incident Response Leadership)

Primary sources for the topics in this module. All links point
to official regulator landing pages, statutes, standards
bodies, professional-institute publishers, or the primary
publication of the reference frameworks and public incident
registries the module studies. The incident-response leadership
discipline the module builds — the classification taxonomy, the
disclosure map, the Article 73 filing runbook, the CxO war-room
coordination, the post-incident learning cycle, and the sector-
share decision — builds directly on filings, statutes,
standards, and public incident registries; a head who cites a
trade-press summary or a vendor blog on Article 73 or SR 11-7
is a head who has skipped the discipline this module builds.
`needs-research` markers below indicate specific facts to re-
verify against the primary source before citing at CxO, audit-
committee, or regulator depth; they are the mechanism by which
the module signals uncertainty rather than guessing at it.
