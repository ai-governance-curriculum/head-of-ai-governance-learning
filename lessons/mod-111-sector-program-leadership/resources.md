# Resources for mod-111-sector-program-leadership (Sector Program Leadership)

Primary sources for the topics in this module. All links point
to official regulator landing pages, statutes, standards
bodies, professional-institute publishers, or the primary
publication of the reference frameworks each sector chapter
studies. The sector-leadership discipline the module builds —
the AI-inside-MRM control alignment, the state-by-state NAIC
map, the four-layer clinical stack, the regulatory-submission
AI overlay, the CAIO minimum-practice compliance, the RSP /
Preparedness / FSF operationalisation, and the operator's
safety-and-reliability framework extension — builds directly on
statute, regulator guidance, standards, and published lab
frameworks; a head who cites a trade-press summary or a vendor
blog on SR 11-7, on the NAIC AI Model Bulletin, on FDA GMLP, on
OMB M-25-21, on Anthropic's RSP, or on CISA / NCSC guidance is
a head who has skipped the discipline this module builds.
`needs-research` markers below indicate specific facts to re-
verify against the primary source before citing at CxO, audit-
committee, or regulator depth; they are the mechanism by which
the module signals uncertainty rather than guessing at it.
