# mod-111-sector-program-leadership: Sector Program Leadership

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 20 hours

## Learning objectives

- Banking — lead AI-governance in the model-risk-management context: Federal Reserve + OCC + FDIC exam coordination on AI/ML model risk under SR 11-7 + OCC 2011-12 + SR 23-4; OSFI E-23 for Canadian FIs; interfacing with the enterprise Head of Model Risk Management (where the head-of-AI-governance sits as a peer or a specialist under MRM); consumer-facing AI obligations (CFPB) and fair-lending (Reg B) shape
- Insurance — lead AI-governance in the state-DOI + NAIC context: NAIC AI Model Bulletin adoption cycle by state DOI, Colorado Insurance Regulation 10-1-1, state DOI exam coordination, external actuarial coordination on AI-driven pricing / underwriting; consumer-facing AI obligations
- Health system — lead AI-governance in the FDA + HHS OCR context: FDA GMLP + PCCP submission leadership on SaMD; clinical-governance interface with the Chief Medical Officer / medical staff; HIPAA breach notification for AI-triggered PHI events; state medical-AI regulation; hospital-accreditation body coordination
- Pharmaceutical — lead AI-governance in the FDA + MHRA + EMA + PMDA context: regulatory-submission leadership on AI-enabled clinical trials, pharmacovigilance, manufacturing; GxP interface; inspector engagement; regulatory-submission cadence coordination
- Public sector / federal contractor — lead AI-governance as the Federal Chief AI Officer designation under OMB M-25-21 (or as the head-of-AI-governance for a federal contractor that services agencies with CAIO designations); AI Governance Board seat under M-25-21; IG relations; agency-head coordination; Federal CAIO Council participation; FedRAMP + NIST SP 800-53 + NIST AI RMF interface; congressional-oversight interface where the agency is subject to it
- Frontier lab — lead an RSP / Preparedness / FSF program (Anthropic RSP, OpenAI Preparedness Framework, Google DeepMind FSF, Frontier Model Forum member charter); White House engagement; US AISI + UK AISI evaluation-access negotiation; industry-body chair roles; public-transparency cadence (annual updates, capability-threshold publications, incident-summary publications)
- Critical infrastructure — lead AI-governance for a critical-infrastructure operator: CISA + NCSC engagement under Secure AI System Development Guidelines; ENISA engagement for EU operators; sector-specific regulator coordination (electricity, telecom, water, transportation)
- Design the sector-adaptation methodology at leadership scope — the enterprise operating design comes from senior-ai-governance-architect (level 50) sector blueprints; the head adapts the sector-specific regulator-engagement pattern, board-reporting cadence, and external-positioning posture
