# exercise-03: Career Path Out of Role Mapping

**Estimated effort:** 2 hours

## Objective

Instantiate the five-route framework from chapter
[03-career-paths-out-of-role](../03-career-paths-out-of-role.md)
against your own onward-career state (or a specified hypothetical
head's onward state). The deliverable is the head's own *personal
succession slate* — the two or three onward routes the head is
actively preserving, the credential and community-leadership
investments that keep each route open, and the annual refresh
protocol with the head's executive coach and a peer head of role.

The deliverable is not a wishlist. It is the artefact the head
takes into their next annual coaching conversation and into the
next career-critical decision (whether to accept a specific offer,
whether to schedule a specific credential, whether to accept a
specific community-leadership seat).

## Prerequisites

- Chapter [03-career-paths-out-of-role](../03-career-paths-out-of-role.md)
  — read and internalised. In particular, the five onward routes;
  the per-route trigger / mechanics / prerequisite / anti-pattern
  structure; and the closing personal succession-slate discipline.
- Chapter [02-career-paths-into-role](../02-career-paths-into-role.md)
  — the route-in context (the substrate the head brought into the
  seat shapes which onward routes are naturally open).
- Chapter [01-certifications-portfolio](../01-certifications-portfolio.md)
  — the credentials each onward route relies on.
- Chapter [04-community-leadership-scope](../04-community-leadership-scope.md)
  — the community-leadership seats each onward route relies on.
- Chapter [05-thought-leadership-cadence](../05-thought-leadership-cadence.md)
  — the visible external portfolio each onward route relies on.
- A specific scenario. Options:
  - Your own current onward-career state (if working the
    exercise in role).
  - A specified hypothetical head with a defined route in (from
    the seven in chapter 02), a defined tenure length in the
    seat, a defined enterprise sector context, a defined
    personal financial and life-stage context, and a defined
    onward-appetite (mid-career high-intensity, later-career
    focus-deepening, near-retirement institutional-leadership).

## Steps

1. **Personal-career-state one-pager.** Half-page scoping:
   current role and tenure; route in; enterprise sector; peer-
   network state (weekly-standing peer relationships with which
   CxOs; standing peer relationships at other enterprises;
   community-leadership commitments currently held); external-
   portfolio state (visible thought-leadership pieces in the last
   24 months; community-leadership seats currently occupied at
   operational depth); personal financial and life-stage context
   (compensation dependencies; geographic constraints; family
   / health considerations); onward-appetite (which of the four
   personal-choice dimensions in chapter 03 dominates —
   scope-expansion, scope-diversification, focus-deepening, or
   ready-to-transition-out).

2. **Route-by-route viability read.** For each of the five
   routes, produce a viability read:
   - **Path 1 (CAIO).** Is the CAIO role plausibly opening at
     the enterprise in the head's likely tenure window? Are the
     three enabling conditions (AI material to enterprise
     value; risk surface stabilised; CxO composition has room)
     approaching alignment? What is the head's current standing
     with the CTO / CIO / CDO / CFO peers relative to a CAIO
     elevation?
   - **Path 2 (CRO / CCO / CPO cross-functional).** Is one of
     the three peer-CxO seats on a plausible transition
     horizon? Is the sitting head an obvious in-house successor,
     or is the enterprise likely to reach externally? What is
     the head's non-AI substrate depth (per the credentials read
     from exercise 01)?
   - **Path 3 (NED / board portfolio).** Has the head begun
     accumulating board seats concurrent with the head-of-role
     tenure? Is the personal-network state (search-firm
     relationships; peer-director introductions) supportive of
     the path? Does the personal financial and life-stage
     context support the compensation shape of a board-portfolio
     career?
   - **Path 4 (public-sector CAIO).** Has the head engaged
     substantively with the public-sector community (AISIC,
     NIST AIRC, Federal CAIO Council for US context; UK AISI,
     EU AI Office for EU context)? Does the head's citizenship
     and security-clearance eligibility support the path? Does
     the personal financial context support the compensation
     differential?
   - **Path 5 (industry-body chair / academic).** Has the head
     built the multi-year visible-contribution substrate the
     path requires? Which specific industry body or academic
     institution is the plausible destination? What is the
     head's current standing there?

3. **Preservation-investment plan.** For the two or three routes
   the head is actively preserving, name the specific investments
   over the next 12-24 months that keep each route open:
   - Credentials to earn or maintain (drawing on the
     sequencing plan from exercise 01).
   - Community-leadership seats to accept or maintain (drawing
     on the portfolio from exercise 04).
   - Thought-leadership pieces to publish (drawing on the
     editorial calendar from exercise 05).
   - Specific peer relationships to build or maintain — the
     path-3 board-search-firm relationships, the path-4 public-
     sector network contacts, the path-5 academic-institution
     contacts.

4. **Explicit-non-preservation reasoning.** For the two or three
   routes the head is *not* preserving, name the reasoning.
   Chapter 03 is clear that not every head preserves every route
   — the choice is deliberate. Per non-preserved route:
   - The specific reason (financial constraint; life-stage
     constraint; substrate-fit; appetite).
   - The specific *cost* of non-preservation (the credentials
     the head is not earning that would open the route; the
     community-leadership seats the head is not accepting that
     would build the substrate).
   - Whether the non-preservation is permanent or is a temporary
     stance the head will re-evaluate at a defined future
     point (e.g. "path 4 is not being preserved during the
     current tenure; will reconsider after year six of head-
     of-role tenure when personal financial position may
     support the compensation differential").

5. **Anti-pattern audit.** For each preserved route, name the
   anti-pattern from chapter 03 and name the specific behaviour
   the head is committing to that avoids it:
   - Path 1: preserving the risk mandate in any CAIO seat the
     head accepts.
   - Path 2: building non-AI-adjacency substrate before
     accepting a cross-functional elevation.
   - Path 3: taking fewer NED seats and executing them well,
     rather than accepting every seat offered.
   - Path 4: taking a federal-CAIO seat only when public-service
     commitment is the interest, not as a resume line.
   - Path 5: playing the long game of consistent contribution
     rather than accelerating with a mismatched appointment.

6. **Refresh-cadence protocol.** Author the specific annual
   protocol the head runs on their own succession slate:
   - Annual review with an executive coach — timing, format,
     material sent in advance, specific questions the coach is
     asked to press on.
   - Annual peer-conversation with a peer head-of-role at a
     non-competing enterprise — timing, format, reciprocity
     arrangement (the peer reviews the head's slate; the head
     reviews the peer's).
   - Trigger-based review — the specific events that trigger
     an out-of-cycle refresh (a CAIO seat opening at the
     enterprise; a NED opportunity introduced; a federal-CAIO
     posting appearing; an industry-body chair seat opening).
   - Documentation — where the slate lives, who has access, how
     confidentiality is maintained relative to the enterprise
     employer.

7. **Trigger-response pre-decisions.** Author pre-decisions for
   the specific offer scenarios that would arrive in the next
   12-24 months:
   - If an internal CAIO seat opens at the enterprise, what is
     the head's default response? (Accept subject to scope
     specification; decline; renegotiate.)
   - If an external CAIO offer arrives from another enterprise,
     what factors would move the answer?
   - If a NED search-firm outreach arrives, what is the default
     posture on accepting the conversation and on accepting the
     seat if offered?
   - If a federal-CAIO posting appears, at what point in the
     head's tenure would the answer change?
   - If an industry-body chair offer arrives, what compensation
     and time-commitment thresholds would move the answer?

8. **Peer-review preparation.** Package the deliverables for
   review by the head's executive coach and (separately) by the
   peer head-of-role. The exercise deliverable is the request;
   the response is out of scope.

## Deliverables

- `personal-career-state.md` — the half-page scoping.
- `route-viability.md` — the five-route viability read.
- `preservation-investment-plan.md` — the 12-24 month investment
  plan per preserved route.
- `non-preservation-reasoning.md` — the defended non-preservation
  stance per non-preserved route.
- `anti-pattern-audit.md` — the anti-pattern-avoidance
  commitments per preserved route.
- `refresh-cadence.md` — the annual protocol.
- `trigger-response-pre-decisions.md` — the pre-decided
  responses to the five common trigger scenarios.
- A peer-review request to the executive coach and to the peer
  head-of-role.

## Acceptance criteria

- The personal-career-state is honest about the personal
  financial, life-stage, and appetite context. A scoping that
  reads as if the head is a purely-professional agent without
  personal constraints fails the exercise; the pre-decision
  discipline the chapter builds is exactly about these
  constraints.
- The route viability read produces one of three defended stances
  per route: preserving actively; not preserving (with reason);
  ambiguous (with the specific factors that would move the
  stance either way). Silent skipping of a route fails.
- The preservation-investment plan names specific credentials,
  specific community-leadership seats, and specific thought-
  leadership pieces per preserved route. Generic "I will invest
  in path 3 by being active in the community" fails; specific
  "I will complete NACD Directorship Certification by Q3, I will
  accept the offered seat on the Ada Lovelace Institute advisory
  board, and I will publish two board-facing HBR pieces before
  the end of the calendar year" passes.
- The non-preservation reasoning explicitly names the cost of
  non-preservation (not just the reason). "Path 4 is not being
  preserved because compensation is a binding constraint" is
  incomplete; "Path 4 is not being preserved because
  compensation is a binding constraint, and the cost of non-
  preservation is that I am not building the public-sector
  network at NIST AIRC or attending AISIC workstream meetings
  even though those attendances would compound my current head-
  of-role work at low marginal cost" is complete.
- The anti-pattern audit names the specific behavioural
  commitment per preserved route. Bland ("I will play the long
  game on path 5") fails; specific ("for path 5, I will decline
  any industry-body chair invitation that requires accepting
  before I have three additional published position papers in
  the topic domain, targeting late 2027 as the earliest
  plausible acceptance window") passes.
- The refresh-cadence protocol names actual dates or cycle
  timing, actual named reviewers, and actual documentation
  arrangements. The protocol is executable — a reader could
  follow it without asking a clarifying question.
- The trigger-response pre-decisions cover all five listed
  scenarios and use the chapter-06 rehearsed-conversation
  discipline (name what the offer would be, name the acceptance
  or decline stance, name the alternative if declining).

## Stretch goals

- Add a *timing* dimension. Chapter 03 notes that different
  routes open on different timescales — path 2 (CRO / CCO / CPO)
  can open on 12-24 month horizons; path 3 (NED) accretes over
  years; path 5 (industry-body / academic) needs a decade of
  visible contribution. Author the head's *timeline read* — the
  specific years in the head's projected career arc when each
  preserved route is most viable.
- Add a *concurrent-execution* dimension. Multiple preserved
  routes may compound (a NED seat opens the substrate for a
  future federal-CAIO candidacy; industry-body chair work
  compounds academic-affiliation eligibility). Author the
  cross-route synergies in the head's plan.
- Add a *conversation-rehearsal* dimension. Draft, in the head's
  own voice, the specific opening lines the head would use in
  each of the trigger-response pre-decisions. This composes
  directly with exercise 05's difficult-career-conversation
  discipline (from chapter 06).

## Notes and starter guidance

- The exercise's most consequential step is the explicit-non-
  preservation reasoning. It is easy to preserve every route in
  principle. It is expensive in reality — every preserved route
  requires investment; investment across five routes is more
  time and attention than the head has. Naming the routes the
  head is not preserving, and the reasons, is what makes the
  plan viable.
- The trigger-response pre-decisions are the operational payoff
  of the exercise. A head who has pre-decided is a head who
  responds to an unexpected offer with a prepared answer. A
  head who has not is a head who is deciding under pressure. The
  chapter-06 rehearsed-conversation discipline is what the pre-
  decisions become when the offer actually arrives.
- The peer-review step is critical. The head's own read on their
  route viability is subject to specific biases (over-preserving
  paths the head finds glamorous; under-preserving paths the
  head is uncomfortable articulating). A peer head-of-role and an
  executive coach both push back on the biases. The exercise's
  deliverable is the request; the actual value is in the
  responses.
- The exercise composes with exercise 05 (thought leadership) and
  exercise 04 (community leadership). The preservation
  investments are drawn from those portfolios. Working the
  exercises in order (01 → 02 → 04 → 05 → 03) produces a
  coherent architecture; jumping in at exercise 03 without the
  substrate exercises produces a plan whose components are not
  aligned.
