# exercise-04: Community Leadership Portfolio Authoring

**Estimated effort:** 2 hours

## Objective

Author the head's *community-leadership portfolio* against the
five-cluster taxonomy from chapter
[04-community-leadership-scope](../04-community-leadership-scope.md).
The deliverable is a specific two-to-four seat portfolio, the
joint-seat protocol negotiated (or to be negotiated) with the
enterprise CEO and General Counsel, the working-group-output
commitments per seat (position papers, comment letters, standards
drafting), and the time-budget reconciliation against the head's
calendar.

The deliverable is not a list of bodies the head is interested in.
It is a specific ratified portfolio the head could present at the
enterprise's annual objective-setting cycle, defend to the CFO in
the credentials-and-community line of the function's budget, and
handoff to a successor at the end of tenure.

## Prerequisites

- Chapter [04-community-leadership-scope](../04-community-leadership-scope.md)
  — read and internalised. In particular, the five-cluster
  taxonomy (international standards bodies; public-sector and
  cross-sector consortiums; industry bodies; academic and policy
  institutions; independent-audit-community); the seat-selection
  principles (anchor / adjacency / credential-community /
  optionally onward-path); the time-budget calibration; and the
  joint-seat protocol.
- Chapter [[mod-106-external-positioning-and-voluntary-codes]]
  chapter 03 on the enterprise's external-positioning portfolio
  — the head's personal seat portfolio composes with the
  institutional portfolio.
- Chapter [01-certifications-portfolio](../01-certifications-portfolio.md)
  — the credentials that gate specific community-membership
  seats.
- Chapter [03-career-paths-out-of-role](../03-career-paths-out-of-role.md)
  — the onward routes the portfolio may preserve (paths 3, 4, 5
  in particular).
- A specific scenario. Options:
  - Your own current seat (if working the exercise in role).
  - A specified hypothetical head with a defined enterprise
    sector (from [[mod-111-sector-program-leadership]]), a
    defined enterprise external-positioning portfolio (which
    voluntary codes signed, which industry bodies joined), a
    defined credentials substrate, and a defined onward-route
    preservation stance.

## Steps

1. **Enterprise-external-positioning read.** Half-page inventory
   of the enterprise's institutional external-positioning
   portfolio (from
   [[mod-106-external-positioning-and-voluntary-codes]]):
   voluntary codes signed (US White House Voluntary Commitments,
   Seoul Frontier AI Safety Commitments, Hiroshima International
   Code of Conduct, sector-specific codes); industry bodies the
   enterprise is a member of; standards bodies the enterprise
   sponsors mirror-committee participation in; public-sector
   consortiums the enterprise is a named-enterprise participant
   in. The head's personal portfolio operationalises this
   institutional stance; identifying it first ensures the
   personal portfolio composes.

2. **Cluster-by-cluster candidate-seat identification.** Against
   the five-cluster taxonomy, list the specific seats the head
   could plausibly hold given their credentials, sector, and
   current standing. Do not commit yet — this is the candidate
   list. Per candidate seat:
   - Sponsoring body (ISO SC 42 US TAG; AISIC; FMF safety-
     research workstream; PAI synthetic-media working group; IAPP
     AIGP body-of-knowledge committee; NAIC Big Data and AI (H)
     Working Group; NIST AIRC generative-AI profile working
     group; ForHumanity EU AI Act audit-scheme working group;
     etc.).
   - Entry mechanism (national mirror committee; individual
     application; named-enterprise representative; invited
     participation; credential-gated).
   - Attribution posture (named individual with enterprise
     disclosed; enterprise representative bound to enterprise
     positions; private-capacity individual).
   - Time-cost estimate (annualised hours per chapter 04's
     budget calibration).
   - Onward-route composition (does this seat compose with
     preserved onward routes; if so, which).

3. **Portfolio construction.** From the candidate list, construct
   a specific two-to-four-seat portfolio. Apply the chapter's
   selection principles:
   - **One anchor seat.** Central to the enterprise's posture.
     Report on quarterly to the audit committee.
   - **One adjacency seat.** Outside the anchor cluster;
     extends perspective.
   - **One credential-community seat.** Maintains active
     credential-body engagement.
   - **Optionally one onward-path seat.** Curated for a
     specific preserved onward route.
   - Defend each choice in a sentence or two. Why this seat
     rather than a substitute in the candidate list.

4. **Joint-seat protocol drafting.** For each portfolio seat,
   draft the specific joint-seat protocol terms to be negotiated
   (or already negotiated) with the CEO and General Counsel:
   - **Attribution.** The specific attribution posture (named
     individual / enterprise representative / private capacity).
   - **Confidentiality.** The working-body's confidentiality
     rules; the enterprise's own disclosure obligations
     conflict-check.
   - **Publication.** Whether working-group outputs the head
     co-authors are published with the enterprise attribution;
     the pre-clearance protocol with Communications and Legal.
   - **Time.** The hours per year committed and where they sit
     in the head's calendar; the accounting treatment (in-role
     hours vs personal).
   - **Ratified positions.** The enterprise-ratified positions
     the head is bound to represent, and the specific process by
     which the head can propose a position the enterprise has
     not yet ratified (typically: the head drafts the position,
     runs it through the enterprise's external-positioning
     governance committee, obtains ratification before
     committing publicly).

5. **Working-group-output commitments.** Per portfolio seat,
   specify the working-group-output commitments over the next 12
   months:
   - Position papers: named lead-author or co-author role, or
     contributor-only role. Where lead-author, name the target
     topic and target publication window.
   - Comment letters: named regulator consultations the seat
     will contribute to over the horizon. Both enterprise-filed
     and working-body-filed contributions.
   - Standards drafting: specific working-group documents the
     head will contribute to (Committee Draft, DIS, FDIS
     review), with target milestones.
   - Working-group participation: standing meeting cadence,
     specific workstream (if the body has multiple).

6. **Time-budget reconciliation.** Sum the annualised hours
   across the portfolio. Compare against the head's total
   available hours for community-leadership activity (chapter 04
   estimates 8-15% of a working year as the typical portfolio
   size). Reconcile:
   - If the total exceeds 800 hours per year: cut a seat or
     downgrade participation-depth on one seat.
   - If the total is under 300 hours per year: identify one
     underweight seat and either upgrade participation-depth
     or add an adjacency seat.
   - Reconcile against other calendar obligations —
     board-preparation cycles, regulator engagements, audit-
     committee cadence, incident-response bandwidth reserve.

7. **Enterprise-portfolio composition audit.** Verify the head's
   personal portfolio composes with the enterprise's
   institutional portfolio. Per portfolio seat, name the
   specific institutional-positioning line the seat
   operationalises. Where a portfolio seat does not tie to an
   institutional-positioning line, either add the institutional
   line (surface the gap to the CEO in the next external-
   positioning review) or reconsider the seat.

8. **Refresh-cadence protocol.** Author the annual refresh
   discipline:
   - When is the portfolio reviewed (typically at the start of
     the enterprise's annual objective-setting cycle)?
   - Who reviews it (CEO; General Counsel; head of
     Communications; head of external-positioning committee)?
   - What triggers an out-of-cycle review (a seat's working-group
     mandate closes; a new seat opportunity arrives; the head's
     onward-route preservation shifts)?
   - How is the portfolio documented (a specific artefact type;
     where it lives; who has access)?

## Deliverables

- `enterprise-portfolio-inventory.md` — the half-page inventory
  of the enterprise's institutional external-positioning
  portfolio.
- `candidate-seats.md` — the cluster-by-cluster candidate list
  with entry mechanism, attribution posture, time-cost estimate,
  and onward-route composition per candidate.
- `portfolio-selection.md` — the specific two-to-four-seat
  portfolio with defended selection reasoning.
- `joint-seat-protocol.md` — the joint-seat protocol terms per
  seat, drafted for negotiation with the CEO and GC.
- `working-group-outputs.md` — the 12-month working-group-output
  commitments per seat.
- `time-budget-reconciliation.md` — the annualised hours
  summation and reconciliation.
- `enterprise-portfolio-composition.md` — the composition audit
  linking each seat to an institutional-positioning line.
- `refresh-cadence.md` — the annual review discipline.

## Acceptance criteria

- The portfolio contains between two and four seats. A single
  seat is under-invested for a head-of-role; more than four is
  over-committed and produces thin contributions.
- Every seat has a stated attribution posture. Ambiguous
  attribution ("participates when relevant") fails; specific
  ("named individual with enterprise disclosed on all working-
  group outputs; enterprise-representative bound to ratified
  positions on public statements") passes.
- Every seat has a stated time-cost estimate that ties to the
  chapter's calibration ranges. Estimates that are unjustified
  or that produce a total over 800 hours per year fail.
- The joint-seat protocol per seat covers all five items
  (attribution, confidentiality, publication, time, ratified
  positions). Missing items fail.
- The working-group-output commitments are specific. Vague ("will
  contribute to position papers as opportunities arise") fails;
  specific ("will lead-author the position paper on frontier-
  model evaluation-threshold interpretation for publication in
  Q3, will co-author the working-body comment letter on the EU
  AI Office implementing act on transparency by the June 30
  deadline") passes.
- The enterprise-portfolio composition audit ties each seat to
  an institutional-positioning line. Where a seat does not tie,
  the exercise deliverable names the specific line to be added
  or the seat to be reconsidered.
- The refresh-cadence protocol is executable. A specific reader
  can follow it without asking a clarifying question.
- Where the head's current portfolio deviates from the ratified
  one (the head is not yet in a seat the portfolio calls for; the
  head is in a seat the portfolio no longer calls for), the
  exercise records the deviation and the specific transition
  plan (join by end of Q2; exit by end of Q4).

## Stretch goals

- Add a *cluster-3-industry-body-sector-map* dimension. For the
  enterprise's sector, expand the cluster-3 candidate list with
  every sector-specific industry body from
  [[mod-111-sector-program-leadership]] chapter for the sector,
  and read each against the portfolio-selection principles. This
  produces a more thorough candidate list at the cost of
  additional preparation time.
- Add a *joint-seat-with-a-team-member* dimension. Some
  community-leadership seats are more effectively co-held with a
  team member (a level-35 evaluator on an ISO SC 42 working
  group; a level-25 engineer on a MLCommons AI Safety benchmark-
  authoring team). Author the specific pairs the head would
  propose to run with the team's development portfolio in
  [[mod-108-leading-the-governance-function]] chapter 03.
- Add a *cost-line* dimension. For each seat, name the direct
  cost (membership dues, conference fees, travel budget) and the
  indirect cost (paid hours). Reconcile with the credentials-
  and-community line in the function's budget from
  [[mod-113-budget-vendor-p-and-l-and-investment-case]]. This
  turns the portfolio into a defensible budget line rather than
  an implicit calendar cost.

## Notes and starter guidance

- The most common failure mode is authoring the portfolio as an
  aspiration list. A five- or six-seat portfolio is a portfolio
  the head cannot execute at operational depth. The chapter is
  explicit that portfolio *depth* matters more than portfolio
  *count*. The exercise's discipline is the ruthless cut from
  candidate list to two-to-four seats.
- The joint-seat protocol is often the exercise's newest work.
  Many heads participate in community bodies without an explicit
  protocol — the participation just happens, and the enterprise's
  read on the participation is implicit. The exercise makes the
  protocol explicit. Where a head is currently participating
  under an implicit protocol, the exercise deliverable is the
  first-time draft of the protocol; negotiating it with the CEO
  and GC is a follow-up conversation the exercise prepares for.
- The working-group-output commitments are what turn seats into
  substance. A seat with no outputs is a seat whose community-
  standing does not compound. The specific outputs (position
  papers, comment letters, standards drafts) are what the
  chapter 05 thought-leadership editorial calendar picks up on.
- The exercise composes with exercise 05 (thought-leadership
  cadence). The working-group-output commitments here become the
  layer-1 or layer-2 pieces in the exercise 05 editorial
  calendar. Working the exercises in order (04 → 05) produces a
  coherent external portfolio; jumping into exercise 05 without
  the substrate seats produces a calendar without a substantive
  substrate.
- The refresh-cadence step is where the portfolio stays live.
  Community-leadership portfolios drift — bodies close working
  groups, new opportunities arrive, the head's onward-route
  preservation shifts. Without an annual refresh discipline the
  portfolio ossifies. The exercise deliverable installs the
  discipline.
