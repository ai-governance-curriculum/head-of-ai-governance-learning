# mod-112-certifications-career-path-community-leadership: Certifications, Career Path, and Community Leadership

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 12 hours

## Learning objectives

- Position the certifications portfolio for a head-of-AI-governance — IAPP AIGP (near-universal baseline), ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Auditor / Lead Implementer (required at leadership scope when running an AIMS), IAPP CIPP/US or CIPP/E + CIPM + CIPT (privacy adjacencies where the reporting line touches privacy), ISACA CISM / CGEIT / CRISC (governance / risk / audit adjacencies), IIA CIA (internal-audit adjacency for audit-path heads-of-role), ForHumanity Fellow (independent-audit adjacency), BABL AI Fellow (bias-audit adjacency) — with the sequencing decision (which to earn before promoting into head-of-role, which to earn after)
- Map the career paths into head-of-AI-governance — from senior-ai-governance-architect (level 50) as the natural technical route; from Head of Model Risk Management with AI/ML scope (banking); from Chief Compliance Officer with AI-heavy remit; from Chief Privacy Officer with AI-heavy remit; from Chief Audit Executive with AI-heavy remit; from senior legal counsel with AI-regulatory specialisation (rarer); from consulting Big Four partner with AI-governance practice (increasingly common)
- Map the career paths *out* of head-of-AI-governance — into Chief AI Officer (level 70) where the role opens up; into Chief Risk Officer / Chief Compliance Officer / Chief Privacy Officer in cross-functional promotions; into board / non-executive-director seats (AI-governance NEDs); into public-sector CAIO under OMB M-25-21; into industry-body chair or academic-affiliation positions
- Design the community-leadership scope — chair or working-group lead at ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42, CEN-CENELEC JTC 21, Frontier Model Forum, Partnership on AI, MLCommons AI Safety, IAPP AIGP body-of-knowledge committee, IIA IPPF working groups, US AISI Consortium; position-paper authorship cadence; conference-keynote cadence; academic-affiliation decisions
- Design the public speaking / thought-leadership discipline — annual conference cadence, industry-panel participation, board-of-advisors positions, media strategy (in coordination with Communications), position-paper editorial calendar
- Rehearse the difficult career conversation — declining a lateral move into a broader-scope compliance seat that would dilute the AI focus; defending the choice to stay a head-of-AI-governance instead of moving into a Chief AI Officer role that lacks the risk mandate
