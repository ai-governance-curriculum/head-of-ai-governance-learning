# Resources for mod-112 — Certifications, Career Path, and Community Leadership

Primary sources for the credentials, career-path, community-
leadership, and thought-leadership disciplines in this module.
Every link below points to the issuing body, the standards
organisation, the accreditation body, or the primary institution
whose scheme or seat the module references. The head's own
credentials plan, community-leadership portfolio, editorial
calendar, and rehearsed career-conversation stances are only
defensible if grounded in primary sources — the module is explicit
that a head who cites a trade-press summary of ISO/IEC 42001
accreditation, or a vendor blog on ForHumanity Fellow status,
is a head whose portfolio is thin under cross-examination. The
`needs-research` markers in the chapters flag specific facts to
re-verify against the primary source before citing at
audit-committee or CxO depth.
