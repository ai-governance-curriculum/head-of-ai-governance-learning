# mod-107-program-measurement-and-maturity: Program Measurement, Maturity, and ROI Reporting

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 14 hours

## Learning objectives

- Design KRIs and KPIs for the AI-governance program at enterprise scope — control-testing pass rate, exception-waiver load, incident MTTR, board-report cycle time, regulator-response latency, third-party-vendor tier coverage, evidence-completeness rate, AIA turnaround time, maturity progression, cost-per-AI-system-governed — with target ranges calibrated to enterprise risk appetite
- Author the enterprise AI-governance maturity model — informed by NIST AI RMF Playbook maturity dimensions, ISO/IEC 42001 maturity dimensions, IAPP AIGP body-of-knowledge competence dimensions, MLCommons AI Safety benchmarking, ForHumanity IAAIS maturity criteria — with defensible tier definitions and an assessment cadence
- Design the annual maturity assessment — self-assessment, second-line challenge, third-line audit input, external benchmarking against peer institutions in-sector — feeding board reporting and multi-year investment planning
- Design the ROI reporting shape to CFO — avoided-loss modelling (regulator-fine avoidance, incident-cost avoidance, third-party-defence cost avoidance), deal-enablement modelling (trust-margin, regulator-approval-speed premium, tender-eligibility uplift), trust-margin modelling (customer-retention delta, employee-attraction delta) — with the discipline that ROI claims are defensible under scrutiny
- Design the program-cost management discipline — headcount, vendor spend, tooling depreciation, external-audit cost, training cost, certification cost, incident-response cost — coordinated with the finance business partner
- Rehearse the difficult program-measurement conversation — telling the board a KPI is red because the program is genuinely behind; telling the CFO an ROI target was missed because the incident-cost avoidance was lower than modelled; defending a maturity tier that peer institutions have surpassed
