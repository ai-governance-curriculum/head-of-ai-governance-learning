# Resources for mod-107 — Program Measurement, Maturity, and ROI Reporting

Primary sources for the topics in this module. All links point
to official standards bodies, regulator landing pages, industry-
body publishers, or the primary publication of the reference
frameworks the module studies. The KRI/KPI portfolio, the
maturity model, the annual assessment runbook, the ROI pack,
and the program-cost pack the module produces are only
defensible if they are grounded in primary sources — a head who
quotes a trade-press summary at the CFO or audit committee is a
head who has skipped the measurement discipline the module
builds. `needs-research` markers below indicate specific facts
to re-verify against the primary source before citing at CFO or
audit-committee depth; they are the mechanism by which the
module signals uncertainty rather than guessing at it.

## Enterprise risk-management and appetite frameworks (chapter 01)

- [COSO — Enterprise Risk Management — Integrating with
  Strategy and Performance (2017)](https://www.coso.org/guidance-erm)
  — the ERM framework the KRI-threshold-against-appetite
  discipline in chapter 01 anchors on and the risk-appetite
  reference the CRO's appetite framework typically extends.
- [ISO 31000:2018 — Risk management — Guidelines](https://www.iso.org/standard/65694.html)
  — the international risk-management guideline supporting
  the vocabulary the KRI portfolio uses.
- [IIA International Professional Practices Framework (IPPF)](https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/what-are-the-standards/)
  — the third-line audit standards the CAE tests the
  portfolio and the maturity assessment against. Chapter 01,
  chapter 03.
- [IIA Three Lines Model (2020)](https://www.theiia.org/en/content/position-papers/2020/the-iia-three-lines-model/)
  — the 2020 Three Lines Model that governs the boundary
  between the second-line challenge process, the third-line
  CAE input, and the head's own function. Chapter 03.

## NIST — AI RMF and the Playbook (chapter 02)

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (AI 100-1)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf)
  — the AI RMF 1.0 base document (January 2023). The four
  functions (GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE) and their
  categories and subcategories are the leadership-shape axis
  of chapter 02's maturity model.
- [NIST AI Resource Center (AIRC)](https://airc.nist.gov/)
  — the NIST landing site hosting the AI RMF Playbook,
  Profiles, crosswalks, and open comment windows. Chapter 02
  (Playbook as maturity input), chapter 03 (assessment
  scoring against Playbook actions).
- [NIST AI RMF Generative AI Profile (AI 600-1)](https://airc.nist.gov/AI_RMF_Knowledge_Base/AI_RMF/Uses/GenAI-Profile)
  — the generative-AI profile extending the AI RMF for
  generative-AI-specific risks. Chapter 02 (axis-specific
  scoring granularity for enterprises with generative-AI
  surface).

## ISO / IEC AI management-system and adjacent standards (chapter 02)

- [ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI management system](https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html)
