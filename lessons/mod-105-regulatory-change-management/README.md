# mod-105-regulatory-change-management: Enterprise Regulatory Change Management

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 14 hours

## Learning objectives

- Design the enterprise horizon-scanning cadence for AI regulation — inputs, cadence, escalation thresholds — coordinating with `ai-governance-analyst` (level 15) on the tracking legwork and with legal counsel on legal-opinion interpretation
- Design the interpretation-and-prioritisation workflow — when a new obligation lands, how the head triages priority across the enterprise portfolio, how the impact assessment is scoped, how the delta is handed to `senior-ai-governance-architect` (level 50) for the crosswalk update
- Design the enterprise change-management plan for a regulatory change — communications cadence (from CEO letter to team-level runbook update), training refresh, exception window management, board-report inclusion, regulator-facing notification where relevant
- Manage the tension between speed (the regulator's compliance date) and safety (the enterprise's operational readiness) — where the head has to argue for a slower rollout, or defend a fast one, with defensible facts
- Lead the coordination between senior-ai-governance-architect (level 50, updated crosswalk), product / engineering (implementation), legal (interpretation), procurement (vendor-contract updates), HR (training refresh), and communications (public trust posture)
- Rehearse specific pending changes as of the research window — EU AI Act GPAI obligations timelines, CEN-CENELEC JTC 21 harmonised-standards publication dates, state AI act enforcement dates (Colorado 2026, Texas TRAIGA), Korea AI Basic Act, OSFI E-23 2027 effective date, evolving AISI methodology
