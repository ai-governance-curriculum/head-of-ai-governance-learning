# Resources for mod-105 — Enterprise Regulatory Change Management

Primary sources for the topics in this module. All links point to
official statute publishers, regulator landing pages, standards
bodies, or the professional associations that maintain the
standard. The scanning system, interpretation workflow, and
change-management plan the module authors are only defensible if
they are grounded in primary sources — a head who quotes a trade-
press summary at the audit committee is a head who has skipped
the packaging discipline this module builds.

## EU AI Act — the primary text and its application timeline

- [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — the EU AI Act (consolidated text via
  EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) — the
  consolidated legal text. Chapters 01, 02, and 06. Article 113 is
  the phased-application-dates article; the head reads it against
  each obligation the enterprise faces.
- [EUR-Lex — Official Journal (OJEU)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/oj/direct-access.html)
  — the citation channel for harmonised standards under the AI Act.
  A JTC 21 standard becomes citation-relevant on OJEU publication;
  chapter 01 tracks OJEU as a source category.
- [European AI Office (EU AI Office landing page)](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-office)
  — the Commission-anchored Union-level body publishing GPAI Code
  of Practice work, guidance, and Commission implementing /
  delegated acts. Chapters 01, 02, 06.
- [EU AI Office — General-Purpose AI Code of Practice](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-code-practice)
  — the Code of Practice work stream. Chapter 06 rehearsal 1.
- [European Artificial Intelligence Board](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-board)
  — the Article 65 Board of Member-State representatives. Chapter
  01 (source category).
- [European Commission — Have Your Say portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say_en)
  — the Commission-wide public-consultation platform, source for
  landing consultations on the AI Act and adjacent instruments.
  Chapter 01, chapter 06.
- [European Commission — implementing and delegated acts register](https://ec.europa.eu/transparency/comitology-register/screen/home)
  — where Commission acts under the AI Act land. Chapter 01.

## Standards — CEN-CENELEC JTC 21 and ISO/IEC SC 42

- [CEN-CENELEC JTC 21 — Artificial Intelligence](https://www.cencenelec.eu/areas-of-work/cen-cenelec-topics/artificial-intelligence/)
  — the European standardisation committee producing the harmonised
  standards under the Commission's standardisation request. Chapter
  01 (source category), chapter 06 rehearsal 2.
- [European Commission — Standardisation request M/593 on
  artificial intelligence (as amended)](https://ec.europa.eu/growth/tools-databases/enorm/mandate/593_en)
  — the Commission's original AI-standardisation request. Chapter
  06 rehearsal 2. See needs-research note below on the current
  amendment status.
- [ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 — Artificial intelligence](https://www.iso.org/committee/6794475.html)
  — the international AI-standards committee. Chapter 01 (source
  category), chapter 06.
- [ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI management system](https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html)
  — the AIMS standard the enterprise's AIMS is built against.
  Clause 8.1 (operational planning and control) frames the
  change-management plan (chapter 03); clause 6.2 (AI objectives
  and planning) frames the compliance-date posture (chapter 04);
  clause 9.1 (monitoring, measurement, analysis, and evaluation)
  frames the KRI monitoring (chapter 04, chapter 05, exercise 04).
- [ISO/IEC 42005:2025 — AI system impact assessment](https://www.iso.org/standard/44545.html)
  — supports the packaging step's impact analysis (chapter 02).
- [ISO/IEC 42006:2025 — Requirements for bodies providing audit
  and certification of AI management systems](https://www.iso.org/standard/44546.html)
  — the certification-body standard the annual scanning-system
  audit (chapter 01) and the plan artefacts (chapter 03) will be
  tested against.
- [ISO/IEC 23894:2023 — Guidance on AI risk management](https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html)
  — supports the compliance-date-posture residual-risk vocabulary
  (chapter 04).
- [ISO 31000:2018 — Risk management — Guidelines](https://www.iso.org/standard/65694.html)
  — the parent risk-management standard whose vocabulary chapter 04
  uses at the risk-committee interface.
- [BSI — Artificial intelligence standards committee (UK mirror
  to JTC 21 and SC 42)](https://www.bsigroup.com/en-GB/industries-and-sectors/artificial-intelligence/)
  — the BSI landing page for UK national-body engagement on AI
  standards. Chapter 01 (source category).
- [DIN — Deutsches Institut für Normung — Künstliche
  Intelligenz](https://www.din.de/en/innovation-and-research/artificial-intelligence)
  — the German national body's AI standards work. Chapter 01.
- [AFNOR — Intelligence artificielle](https://normalisation.afnor.org/thematiques/intelligence-artificielle/)
  — the French national body's AI standards work. Chapter 01.
