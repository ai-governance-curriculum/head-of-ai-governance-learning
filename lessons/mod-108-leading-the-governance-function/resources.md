# Resources for mod-108 — Leading the AI Governance Function

Primary sources for the topics in this module. All links point
to official standards bodies, professional-institute publishers,
regulator landing pages, or the primary publication of the
reference frameworks the module studies. The org chart, the
multi-year hiring plan, the coaching cadence, the independence-
balance discipline, the CAE coordination file, the vendor
account plans, and the difficult-conversation rehearsals the
module produces are only defensible if grounded in primary
sources — a head who cites a trade-press summary or a vendor
blog on the CRO or CAE relationship is a head who has skipped
the discipline this module builds. `needs-research` markers
below indicate specific facts to re-verify against the primary
source before citing at CxO or audit-committee depth; they are
the mechanism by which the module signals uncertainty rather
than guessing at it.

## IIA — Three Lines, IPPF, and CAE guidance (chapters 01, 04, 05, 07)

- [The IIA — Three Lines Model (2020)](https://www.theiia.org/en/content/position-papers/2020/the-iia-three-lines-model/)
  — the 2020 Three Lines Model that governs the boundary
  between the second-line function the head runs and the third-
  line CAE. Chapters 01, 04, 05.
- [The IIA — International Professional Practices Framework (IPPF)](https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/what-are-the-standards/)
  — the internal-audit standards the CAE operates against, and
  the discipline the head coordinates with the CAE under.
  Chapter 05. See needs-research below on the current standards
  edition and update cycle.
- [The IIA — Global Internal Audit Standards (2024)](https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/2024-standards/global-internal-audit-standards/)
  — the current Global Internal Audit Standards on the IPPF
  landing. Chapter 05.
- [The IIA — Chief Audit Executive resources](https://www.theiia.org/en/leaders-learning/positions/chief-audit-executive/)
  — the IIA's landing page for CAE-focused guidance and
  position papers. Chapter 05.
- [The IIA — Auditing Artificial Intelligence practice guidance](https://www.theiia.org/en/content/guidance/recommended/supplemental/practice-guides/auditing-artificial-intelligence/)
  — the IIA's practice guidance on auditing AI, which the CAE
  runs the third-line AI audit programme against. Chapter 05.
  See needs-research below on the current published version.

## ISO / IEC AI management-system and assurance standards (chapters 05, 06)

- [ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — Information technology — Artificial
  intelligence — Management system](https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html)
  — the AI management-system standard on the Harmonised
  Structure common to ISO 9001, ISO/IEC 27001, ISO 22301, ISO
  14001. Chapter 06 (CB relationship and AIMS scope).
- [ISO/IEC 42005:2025 — Artificial intelligence — AI system
  impact assessment](https://www.iso.org/standard/44545.html)
  — the AI system impact-assessment standard. Chapter 06
  (assurance-body engagement context).
- [ISO/IEC 42006:2025 — Requirements for bodies providing
  audit and certification of AI management systems](https://www.iso.org/standard/44546.html)
  — the certification-body standard the CB relationship is
  governed by. Chapter 06 (CB selection, surveillance-audit
  cycle, CB rotation).
- [ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 — Artificial intelligence](https://www.iso.org/committee/6794475.html)
  — the international committee whose published outputs the
  ISO axis of the module draws on for currency. Chapter 06.
- [ISO/IEC 17021-1:2015 — Conformity assessment — Requirements
  for bodies providing audit and certification of management
  systems](https://www.iso.org/standard/61651.html)
  — the general accreditation standard for management-system
  certification bodies, adjacent to ISO/IEC 42006. Chapter 06.
- [ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Information security management
  system](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001)
  — the ISMS standard the AIMS often composes with; joint
  audit-bundle context. Chapter 06.
- [ISO 22301:2019 — Security and resilience — Business
  continuity management systems](https://www.iso.org/standard/75106.html)
  — the business-continuity standard adjacent to the vendor-
  continuity discipline. Chapter 06.
