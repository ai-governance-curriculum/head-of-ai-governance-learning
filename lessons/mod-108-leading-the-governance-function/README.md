# mod-108-leading-the-governance-function: Leading the AI Governance Function as a People Leader

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 18 hours

## Learning objectives

- Design the AI-governance function's org shape — analysts, engineers, evaluators, architects — with the reporting-line decisions (directly reporting versus matrixed from product / engineering / risk / audit), the level-ladder mapping (`ai-governance-analyst` level 15 → `ai-risk-engineer` level 25 → `ai-evaluation-engineer` level 35 → `senior-ai-governance-architect` level 50 → `head-of-ai-governance` level 60), and the succession-planning discipline
- Design the hiring plan — the multi-year headcount ladder, the sourcing strategy (career switchers from audit / privacy / model-risk-management / legal / product / engineering; graduates from IAPP AIGP / ISO/IEC 42001 LI cohorts), the interview loop, the sign-off flow, the compensation-band positioning
- Design the coaching and mentoring cadence — 1:1 discipline, career-development plans, technical-training rotation (through `senior-ai-governance-architect` mentorship, `ai-evaluation-engineer` shadowing, product-team embed rotations)
- Manage the second line's independence from first-line product without operational-distance failure — the head has to be close enough that product engineers see the second line as helpful, and independent enough that the second line can say 'stop shipping' with a straight face; coordinate the balance with the Chief Risk Officer where the second line reports up
- Coordinate with the Chief Audit Executive (IIA Three Lines Model + IIA CAE guidance + IIA IPPF) on the third-line AI audit programme — the head does not run third-line audit; the CAE does; the head is a third-line-audit *subject*, not its owner
- Own the leadership-scope vendor relationship — the GRC-for-AI platform vendor at CxO-account scope; the assurance-body relationship (ISO 42001 certification body, ForHumanity, BABL AI); the vendor-satisfaction pulse; the escalation path when a vendor is materially underperforming
- Rehearse the difficult people-leadership conversation — reorganising the second line, escalating a matrixed peer for governance non-cooperation, terminating a senior team member, defending a hire who challenged product too aggressively
