# mod-109-cross-enterprise-leadership: Cross-Enterprise Leadership and CxO Coordination

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 16 hours

## Learning objectives

- Design the coordination pattern with CISO — deep AI security handoff, red-team-finding routing into SOC signal, AI-specific detection engineering; this role designs the interface (with `senior-ai-governance-architect` at level 50), CISO runs SOC-side runbook
- Design the coordination pattern with CIO / CTO and CDO — AI-platform ownership handoff, model-registry integration, data-governance interface, MLOps-runtime interface into which the governance runtime plugs (`ai-infra-mlops` at level 25 executes the wiring)
- Design the coordination pattern with Chief Privacy Officer / DPO — the head owns AI-scoped privacy; the CPO / DPO owns enterprise privacy; the AIA / DPIA interface, the ROPA interface, the CPPA / GDPR / DPDPA obligations shared or divided
- Design the coordination pattern with Chief Risk Officer — the parent ERM framework the AI risk portfolio composes with; the reporting line where the head reports into the CRO; the appetite-tolerance-limit language shared with the enterprise risk register (ISO 31000, ISO/IEC 27005, COSO ERM)
- Design the coordination pattern with Chief Audit Executive — third-line audit programme, IIA Three Lines / CAE guidance / IPPF; the audit-committee joint appearance rhythm
- Design the coordination pattern with General Counsel — regulator-obligation interpretation (this role packages; GC delivers legal opinion); litigation posture; whistleblower and regulator-complaint intake; SAR / MOU / MRA management
- Design the coordination pattern with CFO — governance-function budget, ROI reporting, capital-planning discipline, contingency-reserve planning for regulator fines / remediation programs
- Design the coordination pattern with CHRO — AI-in-HR governance (hiring-tool bias auditing under NYC LL 144 / EEOC guidance / Illinois HB 3773; performance-management AI governance; workforce AI-training strategy)
- Design the coordination pattern with Chief Compliance Officer — the parent-compliance framework; where the AI-governance function reports up through compliance; where compliance owns the enterprise policy library the AI-policy taxonomy plugs into
- Design the coordination pattern with Chief Communications Officer — public trust posture, media strategy, crisis-communications runbook for AI-incident public disclosure (the head owns the AI-governance substance; CCO owns the delivery)
- Chair the AI governance council — agenda-setting, decision-forcing, dissent-capture, minute-keeping, decision-log discipline, escalation-to-board pathway
- Manage the conflict pattern with product owners — stop-shipping decisions, delayed-release decisions, feature-scope-reduction decisions — with the escalation ladder rehearsed so the ambiguity does not become political
