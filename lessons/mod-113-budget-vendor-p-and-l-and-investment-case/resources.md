# Resources for mod-113 — Budget, Vendor P&L, and Investment Case

Primary sources for the topics in this module. All links point
to official standards bodies, professional-institute publishers,
regulator landing pages, vendor primary sources, or the primary
publication of the reference frameworks the module studies. The
multi-year function budget, the CFO-and-audit-committee
investment case, the four vendor-category P&L reviews, the
assurance-body relationship P&L, the four-axis ROI model, and
the CAIO/CIO/CTO/CDO/CFO coordination file the module produces
are only defensible if grounded in primary sources — a head who
cites a trade-press summary, a vendor blog, or an analyst-house
quadrant graphic in front of the CFO or the audit committee is
a head who has skipped the discipline this module builds. The
`needs-research` markers below indicate specific facts to
re-verify against the primary source before citing at CxO,
audit-committee, or CB-relationship depth; the vendor and
regulator landscape this module studies moves quickly, and the
markers are the mechanism by which the module signals uncertainty
rather than guessing at it.
