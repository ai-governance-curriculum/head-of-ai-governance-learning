# mod-113-budget-vendor-p-and-l-and-investment-case: Budget, Vendor P&L, and Investment Case

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 14 hours

## Learning objectives

- Own the AI-governance function budget line — headcount, vendor spend (GRC-for-AI + AI-observability + AI-runtime security + assurance), tooling depreciation, external-audit cost, training and certification cost, incident-response reserve, external-benchmarking cost, community-leadership cost (travel, dues, speaking) — with a defensible multi-year plan
- Author the multi-year investment case to CFO / CEO / audit committee — programme case (headcount + tooling + external-audit + maturity progression + regulatory-change-readiness), transformation case (moving off spreadsheets onto a certifiable AIMS runtime, replacing point tools with a governance-of-record platform), incident-preparedness case (contingency reserve, incident-response retainer, tabletop exercise budget)
- Manage the GRC-for-AI vendor P&L at CxO scope — Credo AI, Holistic AI, ModelOp, Monitaur, ServiceNow AI Control Tower, IBM watsonx.governance, Fairly AI, Enzai, Trustible, Verify AI — vendor consolidation or expansion decisions, contract renewal negotiation, escalation with vendor-account executives where SLAs slip
- Manage the AI-observability vendor P&L — Fiddler AI, Arthur AI, WhyLabs, Evidently — coordinated with `ai-evaluation-engineer` (level 35) on tooling requirements
- Manage the AI-runtime security vendor P&L — Robust Intelligence, Lakera Guard, Calypso AI, HiddenLayer, Protect AI, HydroX AI, Cranium, TrojAI — coordinated with `ai-infra-security` (level 35) on runtime tooling
- Manage the assurance-body relationship P&L — ISO/IEC 42001 certification body (BSI, PECB, TÜV, DNV, DEKRA), ForHumanity IAAIS auditor, BABL AI Algorithm Bias Auditor, external Big Four audit engagement (Deloitte, EY, PwC, KPMG), specialist AI-audit firms
- Build the ROI case with defensible modelling — avoided-loss (regulator-fine-avoidance, incident-cost-avoidance, third-party-defence cost avoidance), deal-enablement (trust-margin, regulator-approval-speed premium, tender-eligibility uplift, enterprise-customer AI-trust requirements coverage), trust-margin (customer-retention delta, employee-attraction delta), transformation (spreadsheet-elimination FTE-recovery)
- Coordinate with the Chief AI Officer (level 70) on the enterprise-wide AI budget envelope — the AI-governance function budget is a component of a larger AI budget; the head negotiates the governance-function line as a proportion of the whole; where a CAIO does not exist, the head negotiates directly with CIO / CTO / CDO / CFO
