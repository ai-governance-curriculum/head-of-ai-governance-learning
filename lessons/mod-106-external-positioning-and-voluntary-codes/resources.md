# Resources for mod-106 — External Positioning, Voluntary Codes, and Standards-Body Leadership

Primary sources for the topics in this module. All links point
to official standards bodies, regulator landing pages, industry-
body publishers, or the primary publication of the frontier-lab
artefacts the module studies. The voluntary-code register, the
standards-body portfolio, the RFI / RFP runbook, and the
difficult-decision rehearsals the module produces are only
defensible if they are grounded in primary sources — a head who
quotes a trade-press summary at the audit committee is a head
who has skipped the packaging discipline the module builds.
`needs-research` markers below indicate specific facts to
re-verify against the primary source before citing at audit-
committee depth; they are the mechanism by which the module
signals uncertainty rather than guessing at it.

## Voluntary codes and public commitment frameworks

- [EU AI Office — General-Purpose AI Code of Practice](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-code-practice)
  — the Commission-convened, multi-stakeholder Code of Practice
  developed by the EU AI Office under Article 56 of Regulation
  (EU) 2024/1689. Chapter 01, chapter 04 (regulator-RFI standing
  envelope), chapter 05 (declining and withdrawing scenarios).
- [EU AI Office landing page](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-office)
  — the Commission-anchored Union-level body publishing GPAI
  Code of Practice work, guidance, and Commission implementing
  and delegated acts. Chapter 01, chapter 04.
- [G7 Hiroshima Process — International Code of Conduct for
  Organisations Developing Advanced AI Systems (MOFA published
  text)](https://www.mofa.go.jp/files/100573473.pdf)
  — the G7 Code of Conduct text as published by the Japanese
  Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Chapter 01, chapter 04, chapter 05.
- [G7 Hiroshima Process — Guiding Principles for Organisations
  Developing Advanced AI Systems](https://www.mofa.go.jp/files/100573471.pdf)
  — the Guiding Principles the Code of Conduct operationalises.
  Chapter 01.
- [OECD.AI Policy Observatory](https://oecd.ai/) — the OECD's AI
  Policy Observatory, hosting the Hiroshima Process AI Reporting
  Framework and monitoring reporting the G7 Code signatories
  submit against, plus the OECD.AI Network of Experts on AI
  (ONE AI). Chapter 01, chapter 02.
- [OECD AI Principles](https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles) — the
  parent principles the OECD AI work programme operates under.
  Chapter 02.
- [White House — Ensuring Safe, Secure, and Trustworthy AI
  (Voluntary Commitments background)](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/briefing-room/statements-releases/2023/07/21/fact-sheet-biden-harris-administration-secures-voluntary-commitments-from-leading-artificial-intelligence-companies-to-manage-the-risks-posed-by-ai/)
