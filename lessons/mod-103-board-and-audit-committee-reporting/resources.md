# Resources for mod-103 — Board, Audit Committee, and Risk Committee AI Reporting

Primary sources for the topics in this module. All links point to
official standards bodies, government publishers, or the
professional associations that maintain the reference. Avoid
secondary summaries when authoring the artefacts described in the
exercises — the packs the head puts in front of governing bodies
have to be traceable to primary sources.

## Board-facing AI-governance guidance

- [Institute of Directors — AI-governance guidance](https://www.iod.com/)
  — IoD landing site. The IoD publishes AI-governance guidance
  aimed at directors of UK enterprises; look for the AI Advisory
  Board / Chartered Director / AI-guidance publications for the
  current version.
- [National Association of Corporate Directors (NACD) — AI
  oversight publications](https://www.nacdonline.com/all-governance/governance-resources/all-resources/publications/)
  — NACD publications landing page. NACD's AI Oversight Blueprint
  and successor publications are the US-market reference shape.
- [World Economic Forum — Empowering AI Leadership: An Oversight
  Toolkit for Boards of Directors](https://www.weforum.org/publications/empowering-ai-leadership-an-oversight-toolkit-for-boards-of-directors/)
  — WEF's board-of-directors AI oversight toolkit.
- [OECD AI Principles](https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles) — the
  OECD's foundational AI principles referenced across board and
  regulator-facing material.

## Parent risk-management frameworks

- [COSO — Enterprise Risk Management: Integrating with Strategy
  and Performance (2017)](https://www.coso.org/enterprise-risk-management)
  — the dominant enterprise-risk framework in US enterprises. The
  five-component structure (governance and culture; strategy and
  objective-setting; performance; review and revision;
  information, communication, and reporting) is what the risk
  committee's standing agenda is drawn against.
- [COSO — Compliance Risk Management: Applying the COSO ERM
  Framework](https://www.coso.org/) — supporting COSO
  applications guidance.
- [ISO 31000:2018 — Risk management guidelines](https://www.iso.org/standard/65694.html)
  — the international parent risk-management framework.
- [ISO/IEC 23894:2023 — Guidance on AI risk management](https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html)
  — the AI-specific overlay on ISO 31000, referenced across
  chapter 03.
- [ISO Guide 73:2009 — Risk management vocabulary](https://www.iso.org/standard/44651.html)
  — the vocabulary the parent risk framework uses.

## Three Lines Model and internal audit

- [IIA Three Lines Model (2020)](https://www.theiia.org/en/content/position-papers/2020/the-iia-three-lines-model/)
  — the 2020 update to the earlier Three Lines of Defense model,
  referenced across chapter 02.
- [IIA — International Professional Practices Framework (IPPF)](https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/what-are-the-standards/)
  — the professional standards for internal audit; particularly
  relevant to the CAE / head interface and the third-line
  independence discipline in chapter 02.
- [IIA — Global Internal Audit Standards (effective 2025)](https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/2024-standards/)
  — the successor standards architecture.
- [IIA — GTAG series on IT and technology audit](https://www.theiia.org/en/products/publications/)
  — Global Technology Audit Guides relevant when the third line
  is auditing AI systems.

## AI management system and audit standards

- [ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI management system](https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html)
  — the AIMS standard the head's function operates against.
- [ISO/IEC 42005:2025 — AI system impact assessment](https://www.iso.org/standard/44545.html)
  — the AI impact assessment standard.
- [ISO/IEC 42006:2025 — Requirements for bodies providing audit
  and certification of AI management systems](https://www.iso.org/standard/44546.html)
  — the AIMS certification / audit body standard. What the
  certification body samples against when auditing the head's
  function.

## Board-facing AI governance standards

- [ISO/IEC 38507:2022 — Governance implications of the use of
  artificial intelligence by organizations](https://www.iso.org/standard/56641.html)
  — the board-facing AI-governance standard.
- [ISO/IEC 38500:2015 — Governance of IT for the organization](https://www.iso.org/standard/62816.html)
  — the parent evaluate → direct → monitor framework 38507
  specialises for AI.

## NIST AI RMF and reference maturity

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (AI 100-1)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf)
  — the foundational framework, January 2023.
- [NIST AI RMF Playbook](https://airc.nist.gov/AI_RMF_Knowledge_Base/Playbook)
  — actions, transparency-and-documentation practices, and
  reference materials, organised by subcategory. The reference
  the maturity update in cluster 6 of the board pack (chapter 01)
  is drawn against.
- [NIST AI RMF Generative AI Profile (AI 600-1)](https://airc.nist.gov/AI_RMF_Knowledge_Base/AI_RMF/Uses/GenAI-Profile)
  — the GenAI overlay published July 2024.
- [NIST AI Resource Center (AIRC)](https://airc.nist.gov/) —
  landing site.

## EU AI Act and serious-incident reporting

- [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — the EU AI Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj)
  — consolidated text. Article 3(49) defines serious incident;
  Article 72 governs post-market monitoring; Article 73 governs
  serious-incident reporting. Referenced across chapters 01, 06,
  and exercise 05.
- [European AI Office](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-office)
  — the European AI Office landing page.
- [European Artificial Intelligence Board](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-board)
  — the EU AI Board landing page.
- [ENISA — AI cybersecurity landing](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/topics/artificial-intelligence)
  — ENISA's AI cybersecurity publications relevant to
  CISO-adjacent content in the pack.

- [OSFI Guideline E-23 — Model Risk Management](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/model-risk-management-guideline-e-23)
