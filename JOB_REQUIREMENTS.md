## Methodology

1. Sourced the canonical leadership-scope task domains for a Head of AI Governance (level 60, AI Governance family) from the public references catalogued in `.aicg/job-requirements.json → authoritative_references`.
2. Mapped each task domain to (a) the role on our level ladder that should own it primarily and (b) the curriculum module that will cover it.
3. Applied the **ownership rule** — assign primary coverage to the lowest-level role that genuinely requires the skill; higher-level tracks link back rather than duplicate. In particular, deferred:
   - *Down* to [`senior-ai-governance-architect`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/senior-ai-governance-architect-learning) (level 50) for the architectural design authority — the head consumes the architect's control library, policy taxonomy, cross-jurisdiction crosswalk, AIMS design, risk taxonomy + appetite, assurance architecture, evidence architecture, third-party program architecture, post-market surveillance architecture, GRC-for-AI reference architecture, operating model, and sector blueprints as the *operating design* of the program they lead. The head does not re-author them.
   - *Down* to [`ai-evaluation-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-evaluation-engineer-learning) (level 35, Governance family) for release-assurance / audit-trail / regulator-facing methodology depth — this role signs off on assurance decisions; peer executes methodology.
   - *Down* to [`ai-risk-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-risk-engineer-learning) (level 25) for the hands-on engineering craft of AI risk (harm-model authoring, red-team, adversarial-ML, fairness, privacy, guardrail engineering, quantification, MRM engineering, incident RCA). The head *sponsors and challenges*; that role *builds*.
   - *Down* to [`ai-governance-analyst`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/ai-governance-analyst-learning) (level 15) for operational analyst legwork. The head *staffs and directs*; that role *executes*.
   - *Sideways* to [`ai-eval-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-eval-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30, AI Engineering family) for application-layer LLM/agent evaluation-engineering depth; to [`model-evaluation-engineer`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/model-evaluation-engineer-learning) (peer, level 30, ML Engineering family) for benchmark / statistical / judge-vs-human calibration methodology depth; to [`ai-infra-mlops`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-mlops-learning) (peer, level 25, Infra family) for MLOps automation into which the governance-runtime slice plugs.
   - *Sideways-and-up* to [`agentic-safety-engineer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/agentic-safety-engineer-learning) (level 40) for frontier-agent red-team methodology and dangerous-capability evaluation. The head *sponsors and reviews the tripwire / rollback decisions*; that role *owns the methodology*.
   - *Sideways* to [`security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/security-learning) / [`ai-infra-security`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-security-learning) (level 35) for deep ML/AI security at platform scale — the head coordinates with CISO-scope peers on runtime defence integration.
   - *Up* to [`chief-ai-officer`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning) (level 70) for AI strategy, AI portfolio P&L, external positioning at CEO / board-seat scope, and the enterprise AI budget envelope. The head *reports into (or matrixes with)* this role where the designation exists; the head *co-produces* AI strategy but does not own it.
   - *Out of scope* to General Counsel and Chief Legal Officer (this role interfaces with them; GC delivers the legal opinion); to Chief Privacy Officer / DPO where privacy-scope is broader than AI (this role coordinates on AI-scoped privacy; CPO / DPO owns privacy enterprise-wide); to Chief Risk Officer where enterprise-risk-scope is broader than AI (this role reports into or matrixes with the CRO in many enterprises); to Chief Compliance Officer where enterprise-compliance-scope is broader than AI; to Chief Audit Executive (this role coordinates for third-line audit; CAE owns third-line independence); to Chief Communications Officer (this role coordinates on public trust; CCO owns comms); to Security Operations / DFIR (this role designs the interface with `senior-ai-governance-architect`; SOC runs the runbook).
4. Flagged everything that has not yet been validated against in-window postings so the next cycle can demote any requirement whose evidence stays empty.

## Ownership map — quick reference for next cycle

When backfilling postings, use this ownership decision to keep the curriculum from drifting into peer territory:

- **AI Governance Analyst** (level 15, `ai-governance-analyst-learning`) — owns operational analyst work. **The head staffs and directs; that role executes.**
- **AI Risk Engineer** (level 25, `ai-risk-engineer-learning`) — owns hands-on engineering craft of AI risk. **The head sponsors and challenges; that role builds.**
- **AI Eval Engineer** (peer, level 30, AI Engineering family) — owns application-layer LLM/agent evaluation-engineering depth.
- **Model Evaluation Engineer** (peer, level 30, ML Engineering family) — owns benchmark / statistical / calibration methodology depth.
- **AI Infra MLOps** (peer, level 25, Infra family) — owns MLOps automation.
- **AI Evaluation Engineer** (lower specialist, level 35, `ai-evaluation-engineer-learning`) — owns release-assurance / audit-trail / regulator-facing methodology depth. **The head signs off; that peer executes methodology.**
- **Security / AI Infra Security** (peer / next-up, level 35) — owns deep ML/AI security at platform scale. **The head coordinates with CISO-scope peers on runtime defence integration.**
- **Agentic Safety Engineer** (sideways-and-up specialist, level 40, `agentic-safety-engineer-learning`) — owns frontier-agent red-team methodology and dangerous-capability evaluation. **The head sponsors and reviews the tripwire / rollback decisions.**
- **Senior AI Governance / Risk Architect** (level 50, `senior-ai-governance-architect-learning`) — owns the *architecture* of the enterprise AI governance and risk program. **The head consumes this as the operating design of the program they lead.**
- **Head of AI Governance** (this track, level 60, `head-of-ai-governance-learning`) — owns *program leadership execution*: running the program the architect designed, board / audit-committee reporting, regulator engagement, external positioning, program measurement, leading the function as a people leader, cross-enterprise CxO coordination, AI-incident response leadership, sector program leadership, budget + vendor P&L + investment case.
- **Chief AI Officer** (level 70, `chief-ai-officer-learning`) — owns AI strategy, AI portfolio P&L, external positioning at CEO / board-seat scope, enterprise AI budget envelope. **The head reports into or matrixes with this role where the designation exists.**
